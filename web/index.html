    About Regicide

    Regicide is a cooperative card game where players work together to defeat 12 powerful enemies (the Jacks, Queens, and Kings). Use your numbered cards strategically to attack and defend!

    • 1-4 players
    • Defeat all 12 face cards to win
    • Work together and strategize
